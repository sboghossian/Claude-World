Frequently asked

Do I need an Anthropic API key?

You need at least one API key — either Anthropic or OpenAI. Claude World works with both. Your keys are stored locally in your system keychain and are never sent to our servers. Bring your own keys, keep your own costs.

Does Claude World store my data?

No. All your data — conversations, memory, configs — lives in a local SQLite database on your Mac. We have no backend, no cloud sync, no telemetry. The Memory Vault is yours and only yours.

Is it really a city?

Yes. The isometric 2.5D city is the actual interface — not a skin or theme. You navigate it like a game. Each building you click opens a full-featured AI workspace. The city grows as you unlock more zones. It has weather effects (rain, snow, aurora), agent sprites walking the streets, animated data flows between zones, and a particle system with 10 presets. It is genuinely unlike anything else in the AI tooling space.

What models does it support?

Claude World supports the latest Claude models including Claude Sonnet 4 and Claude Opus 4 (via Anthropic), and GPT-4o, GPT-4.1, and other OpenAI models. More models are added regularly. The Council zone lets you query multiple models simultaneously to compare responses.

Can I use it offline?

Fully. The app runs locally via Electron. You only need internet when making API calls to Anthropic or OpenAI. All navigation, memory retrieval, and local tooling works without a connection. Perfect for planes, trains, and focus sessions.
